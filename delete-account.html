Submitting a request does not immediately delete your account. After you submit, QuizPeek admin will review the request. Account ownership may be verified again before deletion is performed.

What data is covered?

The request covers account information, credits and credit transactions, usage records, generated answers linked to the account, payment order records, and other associated personal data, subject to records that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or accounting reasons.

What happens next?

The request is sent to QuizPeek's backend and appears in the admin console. After verifying ownership, the developer will delete the account and eligible associated data from systems under QuizPeek's control and update the request outcome. Some records may be retained where a valid legal, security, fraud-prevention, or accounting requirement applies.

For details about data practices, read the QuizPeek Privacy Policy. For privacy questions, contact baonhq.se@gmail.com.
