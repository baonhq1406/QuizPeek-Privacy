QuizPeek Account & Data Deletion

Official page to request deletion of your QuizPeek account and associated personal data.

Submitting this form sends a request to QuizPeek's backend for admin review. It does not instantly delete your account. The developer will verify account ownership, review applicable retention requirements, and process the request manually. You will receive a request reference after submission.

The request covers account details, credits and credit transactions, usage records, generated answers linked to the account, and other associated personal data, subject to records that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or accounting reasons.

What happens next?

The request appears in the QuizPeek admin console. After verifying ownership, the developer will delete the account and eligible associated data from systems under QuizPeek's control, then update the request outcome. Some data may be retained where a valid legal, security, fraud-prevention, or accounting requirement applies.

For details about data practices, read the QuizPeek Privacy Policy. For privacy questions, contact baonhq.se@gmail.com.
