QuizPeek Account & Data Deletion

Official page for requesting deletion of a QuizPeek account and associated personal data.

This page submits a request; it does not instantly delete your account. The current QuizPeek backend does not provide self-service account deletion. The developer will review the request, verify account ownership, and process it manually.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the email address associated with your QuizPeek account, if you can access it.
  2. Use the subject line QuizPeek account and data deletion request.
  3. Include the QuizPeek account email address. Do not include passwords, verification codes, access tokens, or banking credentials.
  4. The developer may ask for reasonable additional information to verify ownership before deleting the account.

Email a deletion request

Support email: baonhq.se@gmail.com

What data is covered?

The request covers the QuizPeek account and associated personal data, such as the Google account identifier, email/profile details, account settings, credits and credit transactions, usage records, and generated answers linked to the account, subject to any records that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or accounting reasons.

What happens next?

The developer will verify that the requester controls the account, review any applicable retention requirements, and then delete the account and eligible associated data from systems under QuizPeek's control. The developer will reply to confirm the outcome or explain if any data must be retained. Sending this email does not mean deletion has already completed.

For details about data practices, read the QuizPeek Privacy Policy.
