QuizPeek Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. What QuizPeek does

QuizPeek is a browser extension that captures the visible browser tab when the user uses the quiz-answer feature and sends the image for AI analysis.

2. Information processed

QuizPeek may process screenshots of the visible browser tab selected by the user. The screenshot may contain text, images, or other content visible on that tab.

3. How information is used

Screenshots are used only to analyze quiz content and return an answer to the user. QuizPeek does not use screenshots for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

4. Third-party processing

QuizPeek sends screenshot data to its backend service for processing. The backend may send the screenshot to Google Gemini to generate the requested answer.

5. Data sharing

QuizPeek does not sell user data. QuizPeek does not share user data with third parties for advertising or unrelated purposes.

6. Browsing activity

QuizPeek does not collect or store the user's browsing history. The extension accesses page content only as required for its features.

7. Local storage

QuizPeek may store extension settings such as the enabled state and Auto Wayground state in the browser's local extension storage.

8. Data retention

QuizPeek does not intend to retain screenshots longer than necessary to provide the requested AI functionality.

9. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect data processed by QuizPeek. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the QuizPeek developer through the support contact provided on the Chrome Web Store listing.
